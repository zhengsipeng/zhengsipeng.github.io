I build foundation models that let humanoid robots learn from how humans see and act. I'm a Partner at [**BeingBeyond**](https://www.beingbeyond.com), where I lead embodied multimodal pretraining and work with [Prof. Zongqing Lu](https://z0ngqing.github.io) toward general-purpose humanoid robots. Our research spans the **Being-H**, **Being-M**, and **Being-VL** series — embodied foundation models, learning from egocentric human videos, large multimodal models, and humanoid robotics.

Before joining BeingBeyond, I was a researcher at the Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence (BAAI). I received both my Ph.D. and bachelor's degree from Renmin University of China, advised by [Prof. Qin Jin](https://www.jin-qin.com).
